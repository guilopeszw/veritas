## International policy evidence

### INT-01

[Exame, September 14](https://exame.com/brasil/vamos-nos-inspirar-nas-reformas-que-milei-fez-diz-coordenadora-economica-de-flavio-bolsonaro/) attributes the Milei comparison to economic coordinator Daniella Marques at an Esfera event. The article supports regulatory changes, government structure and tax-benefit review while preserving social assistance. This is a coordinator's reported statement, not proof that the candidate adopted every Argentine measure.

[BCRA, December 29, 2023](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/noticias/objetivos-y-planes-para-2024/) states the intended fiscal/monetary framework for 2024, including eliminating fiscal imbalance and monetary financing and changing exchange-rate arrangements. This establishes announced policy, not the causal explanation of every later outcome.

### INT-02

[Argentina Ministry of Economy, January 17, 2025](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/el-sector-publico-nacional-registro-superavit-financiero-anual-por-primera-vez-desde-el) reports a 2024 National Public Sector financial surplus of 0.3% of GDP after interest; the primary surplus was 1.8%. The displayed card's 0.3% is correct. Avoid silently extending this to consolidated accounts of all levels of government.

[INDEC, second-half 2024 poverty report](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eph_pobreza_03_252282AE14D2.pdf), physical pages 4–5, supports population poverty of 41.7% in H2 2023, 52.9% in H1 2024 and 38.1% in H2 2024. Page 4 also reports 51.9% for ages 0–14. Coverage is 31 urban agglomerations. These values do not establish a 2026 rate or isolate policy causation.

### INT-03

[Band, July 8, 2026](https://www.band.com.br/politica/eleicoes/flavio-propoe-acordo-afta-e-promete-relacoes-de-igual-para-igual-com-eua) reports a Washington livestream in which Flávio proposed expanded US trade and referenced the US–Argentina agreement. The original full livestream was not reviewed. Keep this framed as Band's account.

[USTR, February 5, 2026](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/february/ambassador-greer-signs-united-states-argentina-agreement-reciprocal-trade-and-investment) confirms signature of the reciprocal trade and investment agreement. It supports increased market access and lower barriers, not a claim of universal zero tariffs or already completed implementation.

### Narcoterrorism

[VEJA, May 26](https://veja.abril.com.br/brasil/flavio-bolsonaro-pediu-para-donald-trump-classificar-pcc-e-cv-como-terroristas/) reports Flávio saying he asked Trump to classify PCC and CV as terrorist organizations and advocating international cooperation. Suggested replacement: `A reportagem registra a declaração de Flávio de que fez o pedido.` The article does not independently authenticate delivery of that request and does not establish a request for US troops.

[Final official Federal Register PDF, June 5](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-06-05/pdf/2026-11323.pdf), page 34267, confirms both FTO designations, signed May 28 and effective on publication. Prefer this to the scheduled public-inspection PDF. It does not establish that Flávio caused the designation.

[CRS IN11205, March 10, 2025](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN11205), “Policy Context” and “Designation Implications”, distinguishes material-support and immigration consequences, asset blocking, and SDGT-related secondary sanctions. It separately discusses authorizing military force. The existing distinction between FTO and SDGT must remain; do not automatically apply every SDGT effect based solely on this FTO notice. Direct PDF access intermittently failed, but the complete HTML body was retrieved.

[Brazilian Constitution, article 49 II](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/constituicao/constituicao.htm) reserves foreign-force authorization to Congress subject to complementary-law exceptions. [Complementary Law 90](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp90.htm), articles 1–4, provides exceptions and definitions. Thus designation alone does not authorize troops in Brazil; avoid saying every visit requires a congressional vote.

[Lula's criticism reported by UOL, May 29](https://noticias.uol.com.br/politica/ultimas-noticias/2026/05/29/lula-eua-pcc-cv-organizacoes-terroristas.ghtm) must remain identified as his political characterization, not substituted for the legal conclusion above.

## Rally evidence

### Paulista, Lula, October 11

[Folha comparison, October 18](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/10/veja-imagens-comparando-os-atos-de-lula-e-flavio-na-paulista.shtml) reports that the Monitor team did not produce a comparable drone count for Lula's October 11 event after authorization was denied. The campaign attributed this to GSI without explaining the reason in the report. We did not obtain the original request or denial. State the reported absence of a comparable measurement, not an attendance conclusion or personal order by Lula.

### Paulista, Flávio, October 18

The same Folha article reports Monitor/Cebrap–More in Common's 71,900 estimate at 12:20, with ±12% uncertainty: approximately 63,300–80,500. It describes nonoverlapping photo analysis. The original underlying count report/photos were not independently audited.

[Poder360's own estimate](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-eleicoes-2026/ato-de-flavio-reune-76-200-na-paulista-a-uma-semana-do-2o-turno/) is 76,200, based on 11:40 and 12:50 imagery and density assumptions of 1–5 people/m². It describes visibility limitations under trees and marquees. This is primary evidence for what Poder360 estimated, not proof of the true total. Never add the estimates or treat snapshots as cumulative attendance.

### Localities and election results

[Poder360, pro-Lula events](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-eleicoes-2026/atos-pela-virada-de-lula-enchem-ruas-em-dezenas-cidades/) reports at least 37 localities since October 9. [Poder360, pro-Flávio events](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-eleicoes-2026/atos-pro-flavio-encheram-ruas-em-pelo-menos-16-cidades/) reports at least 16 since October 13, but includes Morrinhos on October 11. Its São Gonçalo paragraph dates the event October 15 while its embedded post is dated October 10; the repository already flags this inconsistency. Preserve the exception rather than silently normalizing it.

The site's 43 unique-locality table includes Brasília. Municipal first-round percentages are copied from these reports and were not checked against TSE municipality-level files in this audit. They cannot establish that local votes caused attendance: attendance is not a random sample and participants may travel. Keep this block's direct-TSE review pending.

### October 11 occurrences

[DGABC, October 11](https://www.dgabc.com.br/Noticia/4352198/pm-relata-depredacao-tumulto-e-pichacoes-em-dispersao-de-ato-pro-lula-na-paulista) reproduces a 19:00 SSP account of disorder, graffiti and damage during dispersal and a Metro account with no internal station damage at that reporting point. Temporary station crowd restrictions are separately described. Original agency publications were not located. Neither reported proximity nor clothing establishes perpetrators' affiliation or candidate responsibility.

### October 18 occurrences

[UOL, October 18](https://noticias.uol.com.br/cotidiano/ultimas-noticias/2026/10/18/manifestantes-denunciam-agressoes-de-bolsonaristas-em-dia-de-atos-em-sp.ghtm) describes Butantã and Pinheiros episodes with attributed operator accounts: no injuries/incident filing reported for one episode; declined assistance/police in the other. Political motive was not confirmed, and SSP had not replied by publication. Keep those qualifications. We did not retrieve final police records or a subsequent medical/investigative outcome. This supports reported incidents, not a complete comparative violence rate.

## Closure boundary

The nine blocks have undergone a claim/source comparison. INT-02 has primary-source closure for every listed numerical claim. INT-01/03 and individual rally narratives may be considered checked only as explicitly attributed, dated reports, with the stated limitations preserved. The multi-city electoral table and any claim of independently verified original footage, complete attendance, or complete violence totals remain unfinished. Editorial approval does not remove these boundaries.
