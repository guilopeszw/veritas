## Classification observations

The reviewed exclusions for broad targets (lower debt, interest/inflation outcomes, zero illegal deforestation by 2029 and an investment-destination goal) follow the declared action/instrument counting rule. They should remain visible as political commitments in reader cards even though excluded from this denominator. Likewise, beneficiaries, sector priorities and privacy safeguards can be parameters of an instrument instead of separate counted promises. No count change is recommended on these sampled decisions.

This reveals an important reader-language constraint: the denominator is **distinct implementation actions under Veritas's coding rule**, not an official exhaustive TSE count of every political promise, target, safeguard or value. A percentage should not be described without that scope.
