## Unit rule and accounting

One unit is one independently implementable action and intended object. A single record system with CPF linkage, public/private interoperability, history fields and privacy safeguards is one intervention. A camera system with a million-camera quantity and specified locations is likewise one intervention. Entrepreneurial formalization, training and credit are separate implementable actions. Repeated chapter mentions merge into one canonical ID. Quantities, beneficiaries, safeguards and implementation conditions remain attached and do not earn separate points. Broad outcomes and political values without an independently specified instrument are excluded.

The ledger contains 923 derived units:810 retained actions,76 merged references and 37 context/outcome units. Every original row appears exactly once in `rowAccounting`; split rows point to all derived IDs. There are zero unclassified or unaccounted inventory rows. Each canonical action retains source pages, original inventory IDs, reader-card IDs and merged parameters.

The count is operational: different defensible aggregation rules can produce another denominator. For example, an integrated service platform and the independently deliverable services it enables remain distinct actions; broad outcome targets do not. There is no uniquely natural number of campaign promises.

### Boundary decisions completed in this pass

- Merge repeated Pé-de-Meia, Sport University, Indigenous University, housing titles, agricultural insurance/storage/fertilizers, railway references and repeated platform parameters.
- Keep teacher-degree aid separate from the general teacher salary floor, and digital training separate from a generic employment-platform policy.
- Split telemedicine from home medicine delivery; the debate mentions only telemedicine.
- Split social-program maintenance from management/fraud improvements; the debate promises maintenance.
- Merge a million cameras into the camera-system instrument instead of awarding two points for a system and its quantity.
- Exclude growth, interest-rate, primary-surplus and zero-deforestation outcome targets as separate interventions while retaining their specified instruments.
- Keep the named literacy program; its 80% target is a parameter of continuation, not a separate outcome point.
- Treat source language about a 30-day cancer target and 10% of data as different from network investment and domestic computing-capacity conditions.
