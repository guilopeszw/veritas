# Education and rights proposal verification

Reviewed 2026-10-20: all 31 current proposal cards with topics `educacao` or `direitos`. This is a claim-level documentary pass, retaining the program-attribution evidence already recorded in `final-factcheck-audit.md` and the PT/PL inventories. It does not certify program completeness, authenticate every filing version, grant editorial approval, or change content flags.

## Corrections and source improvements before checking

1. `flavio-educacao`: the source locator must include **physical page 36** for the holiday-school/voucher continuation, not only page 35. The current text is supported; adjust the locator to pp.35–36.
2. `lula-educacao`: the MEC landing link returned only a short non-substantive response. Add [Law 14,818/2024](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2024/lei/l14818.htm), articles 1–3, for the existing incentive and school-trajectory conditions. No numerical future dropout reduction is asserted.
3. `lula-creches`, `flavio-creches-cuidados`: both FNDE landing variants now resolve to an empty landing page. The complete [Resolution 6 of 24 April 2007](https://www.gov.br/fnde/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/legislacao/resolucoes/2007/resolucao-cd-fnde-no-6-de-24-de-abril-de-2007) opens; article 1 supports construction, renovation, equipment and furniture for municipal/DF preschool networks. Keep/add that direct source.
4. `flavio-mulheres-protecao`: replace “segundo a Folha” with direct campaign attribution and add [Brasil por Elas](https://www.flaviobolsonaro.com.br/brasil-por-elas). The page documents the physical center, application and ClarIA. Do not endorse its promised performance.
5. `flavio-creches-cuidados`: primary attribution is available in [PL program](https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2026-content/arquivos/proposta-pl#page=21), physical p.21, supplemented by p.38. Replace Folha-only attribution. Preserve the qualification that the private voucher lasts until a public place becomes available.
6. `flavio-tecnico-fies`: official PL pp.36–37 directly support employer-funded technical scholarships and income-contingent student payments. Add the official source rather than relying on Folha. Do not claim automatic job placement.
7. `flavio-expressao`: official PL pp.66–67 directly supports the announced elimination/revocation agenda; replace El País-only attribution. Use [Constitution article 220 §2](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/constituicao/constituicao.htm#art220) for the censorship rule instead of an entire Constituent Assembly proceedings PDF. The candidate’s description of institutions as censorship remains his characterization.
8. `lula-negociacao-coletiva`: the service landing page is not the clearest evidence for the definition. Add [CLT article 611 and §1](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/decreto-lei/del5452.htm#art611). Conventions involve category unions; agreements can involve a workers’ union and individual employers. Current convention wording is supported, but do not collapse both mechanisms into the same definition.
9. `lula-igualdade-salarial`: add [Law 14,611/2023 article 5](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2023/lei/l14611.htm), specifying **private legal entities** with 100 or more employees. Existing “empresas” is adequate shorthand, but the law is the direct evidence.
10. `lula-fiscalizacao-trabalho`: current general page is a directory and the ordinary complaint service timed out. Add the [MTE October explanation](https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/noticias-e-conteudo/2026/outubro/201clista-suja201d-do-trabalho-analogo-a-escravidao-e-atualizada-com-82-novos-empregadores/), sections on administrative procedure and inspection. It supports rescue, payment of rights, and distinction between a report and an adjudicated infraction. No new cumulative figures need to be copied into the card.
