## Problems with the auxiliary source

[Ceticismo](https://ceticismo.com.br/transcricao/58d1f96a-9fca-43f8-bf8f-e994c679ca16)
reports 1,523 statements, 727 by Flávio and 544 by Lula, but attributes the opening
and debate rules (0:06–3:26) to Flávio. At 0:50, for example, the introduction of
both candidates is attributed to one candidate. These totals cannot measure
participation. The platform itself identifies its classification as AI-generated.

Text retrieval exposed only 40 records; browser navigation redirected to unrelated
advertising. Direct HTTP access returned 403. This no longer blocks corpus
retrieval: the original video's public YouTube transcript panel supplied it.

A [Band report](https://www.band.com.br/politica/eleicoes/flavio-quer-classificar-faccoes-como-terroristas-e-cita-sus-padrao-einstein)
states 500 million prison places, whereas the original captions at 6:16–6:24 say
**more than half a million**. This material discrepancy requires audio verification
before correction/publication. The article embeds `Gqc1rQtzWUk`, not the corpus
video `BgjpOorIXoI`; their timestamps are not interchangeable.

## Conditions for calculation

1. Check passages and speaker transitions against audio; correct captions
   without inventing words.
2. Separate future commitments, historical references, questions, criticism,
   and descriptions of the opponent's promises.
3. Split or explicitly define the denominator: the original 65 Lula and 55
   Flávio cards group measures; they are not a census of all program commitments.
   These are historical snapshot counts, not current totals.
4. Map each measure once, accepting multiple supporting passages without
   duplicating the count.
5. Label the result as coverage of the registered database, with date and a
   measure list. Do not claim whole-program coverage without a complete inventory.
6. Measuring time spent attacking or proposing requires additional temporal
   segmentation; caption counts are not speaking time.
