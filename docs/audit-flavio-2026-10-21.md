## Scope and limitations

All 86 candidate-tagged cards were inspected for wording, attribution, omitted consequences, uncertainty and contradictions. This is a content audit, NOT a fresh primary-source certification of every assertion. Sixteen cards received a new targeted source check below; the remaining cards retain their existing evidence scope and need no automatic promotion of verification flags. No source content was edited and nothing was published.

## Remaining evidence limits

- Complete audiovisual confirmation of debate/interview statements and timestamps was not performed here. Do not mark it complete.
- Earlier declarations need contextual dates and speaker attribution; a team proposal denied later remains a reported proposal and denial, not proof of a secret final policy.

- Not finding a new contradiction is not evidence that a candidate never changed position.
- Missing implementation parameters cannot be filled with inferred intention or a numerical impact estimate.
- A proposal may confer benefits and impose costs; descriptions should show both when documented, without manufacturing symmetry.
