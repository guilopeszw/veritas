## Copacabana: two historical purchases and sales

- Page 25 says Flávio and Fernanda bought two apartments on 27 November 2012, with stated deed prices R$140,000 and R$170,000.
- Page 29 note 52 lists subsequent sales: first for R$550,000 on 24 February 2014; second for R$573,000 on 22 November 2013. These support the chronology reported by Folha already recorded in `flavio-property-crosscheck.md`; this is still the complaint's account of deeds, not direct inspection of deeds.
- Pages 25–26 reference **Annex V of PIC/MPRJ 2018.00452470** for land certificates and notarial acts. The relevant pages do not reproduce complete deeds/current registry certificates. Avoid publishing unit numbers: the complaint's section text and tables inconsistently swap the two unit numbers.
- Page 31's prosecution table itemizes four checks: R$52,500 and R$47,500 on 6 November 2012 (R$100,000 deposit); R$117,500 and R$92,500 on 27 November 2012 (R$210,000 balance). Total R$310,000, matching the two reported deed prices.
- **Page 34 visually reproduces the last two checks** payable to the broker. Their written values R$117,500 and R$92,500 and date 27 November 2012 are legible. These are actual embedded photocopies, unlike the table on p31. Do not say all four checks were visually authenticated; only these two were inspected as photocopies.
- **Pages 34–35 reproduce annotated teller tapes** showing R$38,400 and R$600,000 cash deposits to the broker's account. Page 31 marks origin of those cash entries “n/c.” The attribution of these R$638,400 to the couple and to an extra-deed property payment is the prosecution's inference, not a payer identity demonstrated on the reproduced tape. P25 says R$638,000 while pp31–35 consistently itemize R$638,400; use the latter only as the prosecution's itemized allegation.
- No complete purchase/sale deed, seller discharge receipt, independent identification of the cash provider, or complete account trail was retrieved. Thus the historical reporting is more specifically documented, but not fully independently reconciled.

## Barra da Tijuca apartment

- P36 notes 61–63 itemize R$50,000 and R$1,425,250 checks, R$7,813.04 and R$139,750 transfers, and R$935,000 financing transfer. These total **R$2,557,813.04**, R$7,813.04 above the stated price. Do not silently treat the sum as a fully reconciled price; no source here resolves whether that difference is charges/adjustment.
- P37 refers to a R$1,074,750 loan, distinct from the R$935,000 transfer itemized on p36. Full loan agreement/disbursement statements are needed to reconcile.
- P37 visually reproduces a transaction table for the seller containing ten net R$3,000 deposits in October/November2014, plus a R$3,100 entry and equal reversal (net zero). The displayed rows are labelled “DEPÓSITO ONLINE”; they do not independently identify the depositor or establish cash origin. ## Proposed concise Portuguese copy

### Dois apartamentos em Copacabana — registros históricos

A denúncia de2020 do MP-RJ descreve compras por R$140mil e R$170mil, em novembro de2012, e vendas por R$550mil e R$573mil em2014 e2013. Esses imóveis foram vendidos, segundo a cronologia do documento; não são apresentados como patrimônio atual.

**O que encontramos:** a denúncia reproduz dois cheques de R$117,5mil e R$92,5mil, parte dos R$310mil que o MP relacionou aos preços das compras. Também reproduz registros de R$638,4mil em depósitos em espécie na conta do intermediário. A acusação ligou esse dinheiro a pagamentos adicionais do casal; os registros reproduzidos não identificam Flávio como a pessoa que forneceu o dinheiro vivo.

**O que falta:** escrituras completas, matrículas e documentos que liguem cada pagamento à compra e à origem do dinheiro. A denúncia foi rejeitada em2022 após anulações de provas. Seu conteúdo descreve a acusação da época, não uma condenação.

### Apartamento na Barra — preço, financiamento e móveis

O MP relatou uma compra por R$2,55milhões em2014, com cheques, transferências e financiamento. A denúncia também atribuiu R$30mil em depósitos ao pagamento de móveis, com base no relato do vendedor. As reproduções bancárias mostram depósitos, mas não identificam por si só quem os realizou. Ainda faltam escritura completa, contrato e extratos para conciliar todos os valores. Não somamos o preço histórico aos valores declarados ao TSE para calcular patrimônio atual.
