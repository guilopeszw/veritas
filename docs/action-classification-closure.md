## Scope and result

Read every current excluded-context-or-goal entry and every merged reference, comparing their semantic objects with the canonical targets and the recovered PDF clauses. This is a textual classification review, not direct listening or a certification of historical factual statements. The source corpus is the page-indexed extraction of the recovered PT and PL replicas (`work/program-final-replicas/corpus-paginas.json`), corroborated as described in `program-final-attempt.md`.

**No further clear classification defect requiring an additional denominator change was identified in these entries.** The two newly discovered Lula measures documented separately remain additions to the source inventory. “Excluded” here means excluded from the operational action denominator, not irrelevant, false, or absent from the public proposal summaries.

Primary sources: [PT program](https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2026-content/arquivos/proposta-pt), [PL program](https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2026-content/arquivos/proposta-pl). Physical-page references are used.

## Borderline cases resolved

- **PL-152, p40:** the introductory Paralympic-inclusion goal is followed by concrete talent detection and entity-autonomy measures. Those are retained as PL-155:a/b; reinstating the umbrella would double count the same policy family.
- **PL-257, p56:** increasing Brazilian AI patent participation is an output goal. Public procurement, GovTech and research investments are the stated instruments, retained elsewhere. No separate patent-registration intervention is specified.
- **PL-285:a/b, pp60–61:** the Northeast's clean-energy/data-center advantages are described as regional potential. They do not introduce an additional implementing instrument beyond the energy and technology measures.
- **PL-247, p55:** coordination between Agriculture and Mines/Energy is the execution arrangement of SAF/biomass commitments, not a separately specified public program.
- **PL-123, p35:** attention to struggling regions is a safeguard in results-linked school funding, not a second funding system.
- **PL-163:a/b, p41:** transparency and free expression qualify cultural incentives; there is no separate transparency mechanism in that clause.
- **PL-170, pp43–44:** lower labor cost is the objective followed by first-job and older-worker contracts; those concrete instruments are retained.
- **PT-033-01:a/b, p24:** child public-service/play access is a broad goal, without a new independently specified implementing instrument in that passage. Existing child-service measures remain counted.
- **PT-189-01, p66:** mitigating international price volatility is an outcome/continuity statement, not a newly specified pricing mechanism. It should remain visible as a position without being counted as a separate operational action.
- **PL-109→PL-107, p33:** public gambling-risk campaigns are retained within financial-education/risk-awareness. Under the current integrated-instrument rule, this is a component rather than a dropped action. A different finer-grained segmentation could split it, but that would require consistent resegmentation of both programs, not an ad hoc correction.
- **PL-234→PL-231, p53:** international carbon-credit sales are expressly the intended operation of RenovaBio expansion; the source presents these together.
- **PL-332→PL-317:b, pp69/71:** repeated budget-process reform; the broader budget-management paragraph does not establish a second distinct reform instrument.
- **PL-328:b→PL-105:** both are the same debt-reduction outcome. Canonical PL-105 remains excluded; neither counts in the denominator. This special case has already been documented in the ledger.
