## 1. Six-day working week

Text consulted directly: [Senate — EC 141](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/44184944/publicacao/44189027).

Article 6 establishes two working-hour stages; article 10 delays the effect of
two rest days. Article 2 protects wages. Article 7 allows negotiation of daily
distribution; articles 8 and 9 cover an employee exception and public-contract
transition. The current summary matches the text but must keep these qualifications
visible when expanded. Congress's presiding boards promulgate the amendment;
do not attribute promulgation to the President.

Suggested meaning: Congress promulgated the amendment on October 16; the reduction
is gradual and deadlines run from publication. User-facing wording remains Portuguese.

## 3. Criminal groups and the United States

Strongest primary source found: [published Federal Register, page 34267, notice 13030](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-06-05/pdf/2026-11323.pdf).
Signed May 28 and published June 5, the act designates PCC and CV as FTOs under
INA section 219. It does not order troops to be sent. Prefer the published copy
to the currently cited [pre-publication file](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-11323.pdf).

[LC 90/1997](https://planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp90.htm), articles 1–3,
sets authorization rules and exceptions for foreign forces' transit or presence.
It does not prove that unlawful intervention is physically impossible, nor remove
the need to analyze political risks separately.

[VEJA, May 26](https://veja.abril.com.br/brasil/flavio-bolsonaro-pediu-para-donald-trump-classificar-pcc-e-cv-como-terroristas/)
reproduces Flávio's statement that he requested designation and supported
international agreements. It supports attribution of the statement, not an
official request document. Recommended meaning: according to VEJA's reproduced
statement, Flávio asked Trump to classify PCC and CV as terrorists; that reported
request is not, by itself, a request for troops.

Outstanding: [CRS IN11205 PDF](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IN/PDF/IN11205/IN11205.4.pdf)
and HTML failed to open. Detailed FTO and SDGT sanctions require accessible primary
support; do not conflate the designations. Attempted US Code URLs also returned
missing pages. These failures do not alter evidence of the GovInfo designation.
