# Security and institutions: contextual fact-check

Checked 20 October 2026. This audit checks historical and legal context in the 23 original proposal blocks, not whether promised policies will succeed. Two subsequently added PT blocks contain only attributed commitments and remain covered by the separate PT program inventory. Program-attribution verification must be combined with this audit; a government announcement does not prove an outcome.

## Findings and integration

No substantive contradiction was found in the contextual claims listed below. They are eligible for context verification with the specified source additions. Keep implementation effects conditional. Do not turn lack of a cost estimate in our dataset into a claim that none exists anywhere.

Required source improvements:

- `lula-cameras-prisoes`: add the STF homologation report below. Homologation occurred on 18 December 2024; the judgment was published in February 2025 and implementation runs in 2025–2027. Current text does not give an incorrect homologation date. Do not rewrite it as homologated in 2025.
- `lula-seguranca-constitucional`: add the Câmara PEC record below because the Senate record timed out. Its author is the Executive and presentation was 24 April 2025. Distinguish the PEC from enacted Law 15,358/2026.
- `flavio-seguranca`: replace the Federal Register public-inspection preview with the final official issue; distinguish reported speech that he requested designation from independent proof of transmitting the request. The current wording “afirmou ter pedido” is appropriate. The separate narcoterrorism block still needs the correction specified in the context audit if not already applied.
- `flavio-federalismo`: the official PL program on physical page 69 can replace reliance on the newspaper for the commitment. Add the Constitution for the autonomy claim.
- `flavio-reconhecimento-facial`: direct ANPD page returned an access error, but its official indexed article and the official regulatory monitoring report confirm the 2025 biometric consultation. This supports consultation, not approval of unrestricted facial recognition.
