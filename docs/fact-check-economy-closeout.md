## Required source and wording updates

- **`flavio-ajuste-fiscal`:** replace the older Folha-only “new spending ceiling” formulation with the current official PL wording (physical p.71): “O programa propõe reformular as regras fiscais para estabilizar e reduzir a dívida, obter superávits primários, limitar crédito subsidiado pelo Tesouro e controlar gastos discricionários dos três Poderes.” Suggested title: “Regras para os gastos e a dívida pública”. A mathematical ceiling formula was not found in this passage. Do not imply one was checked. [Official program](https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2026-content/arquivos/proposta-pl#page=71).
- **`flavio-privatizacoes`:** PL p.70 directly supports restarting the PND with case-by-case assessment; replace UOL-only attribution with the primary program.
- **`flavio-negociacao-trabalho`:** PL pp.44–45 supports negotiated terms and platform-worker protection. Use direct attribution, keeping the distinction between individual and collective negotiations. Existing CLT article 611-A does not make them identical.
- **`flavio-empreendedorismo`:** PL pp.45–46 directly supports Minha Primeira Empresa, Sistema S, Caixa and BNDES/Apex support. Replace SBT-only attribution. Add [Law 13,874/2019](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2019-2022/2019/lei/l13874.htm) for the existing framework.
- **`flavio-reforma-tributaria`:** PL pp.71–72 supports the proposed revision and reduction of exceptions; replace secondary-only attribution. Planalto LC214 timed out twice, but the [Câmara official legislative record](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leicom/2025/leicomplementar-214-16-janeiro-2025-796905-norma-pl.html) opened and confirms IBS/CBS/IS and subsequent amendments. Add this working source.
- **`lula-renda`:** add [Law 15,077/2024 article 4](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2024/lei/l15077.htm). The 2025–2030 real increase is bounded by the actual fiscal-rule index, whose maximum is 2.5%; it is not an unconditional 2.5% real rise. Current maximum language is supportable; clearer copy: “Entre 2025 e 2030, o ganho real também fica limitado pelo índice da regra fiscal, que pode chegar a 2,5%.”
- **`lula-regra-fiscal`:** add [LC200 article 5](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp200.htm). The historical 2024 announcement page returned 401; either retain pending for that sentence or replace it with the directly supported fact “A Lei 15.077/2024 vinculou o ganho real do salário mínimo ao índice da regra fiscal.” This closes the historical claim without relying on inaccessible reportage.
- **`flavio-emprego`:** add [MP905/2019](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2019-2022/2019/mpv/mpv905.htm) and [MP955/2020](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2019-2022/2020/mpv/mpv955.htm) for creation/revocation. The 2019 scheme covered ages18–29, unlike the current campaign’s18–24; the card correctly avoids claiming the designs are identical.
