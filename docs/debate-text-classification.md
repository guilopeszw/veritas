## Why this is not yet an atomic official-program coverage percentage

The reconciliation inventory is an extraction-to-card traceability ledger, not a normalized count of commitments. Its796 rows include14 repeat links, compound promises, enabling conditions, beneficiaries and contextual passages. Subtracting14 does not produce782 equally weighted proposals. Some debate measures are campaign promises absent from that inventory; adding them only to the numerator or retroactively to the denominator would change the question being measured.

A defensible atomic-program metric requires a frozen program version, a published unit rule (one distinct action + intended object, with conditions retained), deduplication across chapters, separation of diagnosis from commitment, and independent coding of partial or complete matches. Each selected utterance then needs mappings to these atomic IDs. The current review provides no such final denominator; it must not be relabeled as coverage of the complete official programs. The immediate publishable metric after audiovisual verification is explicitly “share of the proposals registered as cards on Veritas for which at least one measure was mentioned,” with the snapshot86/62 and the partial-match rule visible. It is not a score for depth, feasibility, honesty or proportion of speaking time.
