## Material corrections

### Application workers — `lula-trabalho-apps`

The existing card mentions minimum remuneration, contributions and disagreement, but omits two concrete limits of the government's original 2024 proposal. The [original PLP 12/2024](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/projetos/ato_2023_2026/2024/plp/plp-012.htm), articles 3 and 9, classifies qualifying four-wheel passenger drivers as autonomous workers and counts only accepted trips toward the hourly floor. It does not cover delivery workers. Proposed social-security coverage must not be presented as automatic CLT employment rights. These are properties of the original proposal, not a claim that it became law or that it determines all future platform rules.

Suggested summary:

> Promete ampliar a proteção de quem trabalha por aplicativos. O projeto enviado pelo governo em 2024, porém, propôs motoristas como autônomos e deixou o tempo de espera fora do piso por hora.

Suggested history/impact:

> No projeto original de 2024, o piso contava apenas o tempo entre aceitar a viagem e deixar o passageiro no destino. Parte do valor cobria combustível e outros custos; não era renda líquida. A proposta previa contribuição previdenciária e negociação coletiva, mas não transformava automaticamente o motorista em empregado com todos os direitos da CLT. Ela não abrangia entregadores. Isso limita o alcance da promessa de proteção e precisa ser distinguido das regras futuras ainda em discussão.

[Agência Câmara, 7 March 2024, updated 17 April](https://www.camara.leg.br/noticias/1041667-proposta-do-executivo-regulamenta-o-trabalho-de-motorista-de-aplicativo-prioridade) corroborates the original design. A [Chamber committee report](https://www2.camara.leg.br/atividade-legislativa/comissoes/comissoes-permanentes/cvt/relatorios-de-atividades/relatorio-de-atividades-cvt-2024/view) records drivers' objections to costs and participation. Attribute objections to their speakers; do not convert them into a measured loss affecting every driver.

### Minimum wage — `lula-renda`

The cap was already in expanded copy, but hidden by the unqualified positive summary. [Law 15.077/2024, article 4](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2024/lei/l15077.htm) ties real increases in 2025–2030 to the fiscal-rule index. The root reviewer is handling the summary and duplicated contexts. This is a documented limitation adopted during Lula's government, rather than evidence that the minimum was nominally frozen.

### Social protection — `lula-protecao-renda`

The continuity-only summary omits an actual tightening during the current administration. [MDS Ordinance 1.084/2025](https://aplicacoes.mds.gov.br/snas/regulacao/visualizar.php?codigo=6929), opened in full, changed the protection period for new entrants to 12 months, with two months for specified stable incomes; older entrants retained up to 24 months. It retained half the benefit during transition. This is a historical policy decision, not an assertion that those are necessarily the consolidated rules today.

Suggested historical addition:

> O governo também reduziu a duração da transição para novas famílias em 2025: a Portaria 1.084 fixou até 12 meses recebendo metade do benefício após aumento da renda, preservando até 24 meses para quem já estava na regra. Para rendas estáveis previstas na portaria, o prazo era de dois meses. A decisão encurtou o apoio durante a transição; não extinguiu o Bolsa Família.

Current-period warning: the search index for an [MDS protection page](https://www.gov.br/mds/pt-br/acoes-e-programas/bolsa-familia/paginas/regra-de-protecao/regras-de-protecao) displays 18 months, but opening it redirects to restricted content. The [public FAQ](https://www.gov.br/mds/pt-br/acoes-e-programas/bolsa-familia/conheca-as-regras/Conheca-as-Regras) and 2025 normative text say 12. Do not publish a definitive current-period comparison until the consolidated rule is resolved; do not use a search snippet as final proof. This does not block stating the dated 2025 change.

[Law 15.077/2024, articles 1–2](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2024/lei/l15077.htm) also imposed biometric/CadÚnico requirements, with access exceptions and notification before suspension. If added, show both the requirement and safeguards. Do not say every beneficiary immediately lost coverage.

### Working time — `lula-jornada`

The source is accessible: [EC 141 in the Senate](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/44184944/publicacao/44189027). Besides the general transition, article 8 excludes certain highly paid university-qualified employees from working-time and time-control rules, subject to the text's exceptions. Article 9 provides a different implementation timing for workers allocated to existing public-service contracts. The short summary can remain concise, but the expanded explanation should flag that the timetable and coverage are not identical for every worker. Attribute promulgation to Congress, not a presidential sanction.
