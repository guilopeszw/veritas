# Health verification closeout

Reviewed on 2026-10-20. This supplements `fact-check-saude.md`; it does not replace its primary-source evidence. Scope: 18 current health proposal blocks, including the subsequently added PL commitments. No content status or editorial approval is changed by this report.

## Closed documentary gaps

- **RNDS founding rule:** the complete [Portaria 1,434 of 28 May 2020](https://bvsms.saude.gov.br/bvs/saudelegis/gm/2020/prt1434_01_06_2020_rep.html) now opens. Article 254-A establishes RNDS, including public/private integration and continuity of care; paragraph 3 explicitly describes gradual integration. Article 254-C subjects access to LGPD and access-to-information legislation. This closes the earlier unavailable-text blocker. It does not establish universal implementation or unrestricted access.
- **Mais Médicos training:** the [official FAQ](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/composicao/sgtes/mais-medicos/faq), Formation section, requires a training offer and explains 36 service hours plus eight training hours under Portaria MS/MEC 604/2023. The current card has already removed the previously unsupported training detail. Its narrower recruitment/replacement description is supported by the earlier documented selection source. Training need not be reintroduced.
- **General cancer network:** the [Ministry cancer-care page](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/composicao/saes/atencao-ao-cancer), Treatment section, describes UNACON and CACON as hospital services within the existing SUS oncology network. This closes the earlier concern that the supporting page covered breast cancer only. It does not establish a world-largest ranking.
- **PL additions:** official [PL program](https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2026-content/arquivos/proposta-pl), physical page 39, explicitly includes an elderly companion application with danger alerts, autism-spectrum reference centers, early ADHD diagnosis/support, depression/anxiety care, and care for elderly people with Alzheimer’s and other neurological conditions and their caregivers. A search for the word “autismo” misses the wording “Transtorno do Espectro Autista”; this was checked in context. These are commitments, not established effectiveness findings.

## Boundaries that remain

- Textual examination of official program PDFs supports these attributions; it is not a visual-page audit or authenticated filing/version history. Keep those limitations in the internal program-coverage audit, and do not claim exhaustive program verification from these 18 cards.
- Statements about staffing, facilities, interoperability, contracts and access describe implementation dependencies. They are not demonstrated causal outcomes. No card should convert “could” into a forecast of fewer deaths, shorter queues or guaranteed access without outcome evidence.
- “We do not have targets/costs” means absence from the material held by Veritas, not proof that a candidate has never published such figures. Do not generate a “no proposal” verdict from this audit.
- Editorial approval remains separate. This report does not authorize publication or comparison sharing while the broader completeness gate is open.
