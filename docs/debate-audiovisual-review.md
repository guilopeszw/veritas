# Debate evidence and coverage — 20 October 2026

## Result and scope

The conservative, frozen **card-level** metric is Lula 8/86 (9.3%) and Flávio 12/62 (19.4%). A card enters once when at least one of its measures is explicitly proposed or continued. This is not the percentage of all atomic commitments in the official programs. It is not a speaking-time or truthfulness score. Partial matches and overlapping cards make its denominator unsuitable for an unqualified candidate ranking.

All 903 Rádio Bandeirantes caption segments and all 367 Aos Fatos narrative records were reviewed. A new local transcription of the original audio (MLX Whisper large-v3-turbo, Portuguese, word timestamps) corroborated the 20 selected measures. Extracted video frames confirmed the speaker for each selected interval. This was **machine-assisted audio/text and visual verification, not independent human listening**. Paraphrases are used; uncertain ASR wording is not promoted to a verbatim quote. Final editorial approval remains pending.

## Exclusions and completeness

Fourteen ambiguous mappings are documented in [text classification](debate-text-classification.md). Future references without an identifiable action are excluded. Historical achievements and criticism of opponents do not count as new proposals. Two concrete commitments without exact matches in the frozen catalog are displayed separately: Lula’s debt/Pix proposal and Flávio’s child-sex-offender registry. The timeline also records working-week positions outside this denominator. Missing matches do not prove absence.

A whole-program percentage would require a separately normalized, deduplicated commitment denominator. The 796 extraction rows are not that denominator; subtracting repeat links does not solve compound measures and diagnostic passages.
